# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11)
First public release, generalized from a personal tracker.
* User-entered medicine list with start/stop dates and dose changes that preserve history
* Medicine Guide built from the user's own notes and care-team cautions
* File-based sync for Mac, iPhone and iPad (iCloud Drive), with merge and optional auto-sync in Chrome/Edge
* Installable offline app (web app manifest + service worker); bundled fonts, no third-party requests
* First-run welcome with medical disclaimer; sample-data mode for demonstrations
* Generic defaults: sodium 2,300 mg/day, fluid 2,000 mL/day, BP and SpO₂ thresholds, all editable
