# Medical disclaimer

The Daily Health Ledger is a **personal record-keeping tool**. It helps you write down measurements and habits that you and your care team already track.

* It is **not a medical device** and has not been reviewed or cleared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or any other regulator.
* It does **not** diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and it does **not** give medical advice.
* Alert thresholds and colors are reminders based on numbers **you** enter. They are not clinical judgments. A missing alert does not mean you are well.
* Medicine notes are whatever you type in. The app does not check doses or drug interactions. Ask your pharmacist.
* Nutrition figures, including any estimates produced by an AI assistant, are approximations.
* Never start, stop, or change a medicine because of anything shown in this app without talking to your prescriber.

**In an emergency, call 911** (or your local emergency number).
