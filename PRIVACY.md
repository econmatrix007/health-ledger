# Privacy

**Short version: your health data never leaves your devices unless you move it yourself.**

* **No server, no account.** The app is a static web page. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
* **Where data lives.** Entries are saved in your browser's local storage on each device. If you clear Safari's website data, or delete the home-screen app, that device's copy is erased. Keep a sync file or backup.
* **Sync and backup files** are ordinary JSON files that you save yourself, usually in your own iCloud Drive. Apple's iCloud terms govern those files. Anyone who can open the file can read it.
* **No analytics, cookies, ads, or tracking.** Fonts and icons are bundled with the app, so no third-party requests are made.
* **GitHub Pages** delivers the app's files and, like any web host, may log visitors' IP addresses. It never receives your entries. See GitHub's privacy statement.
* **AI assistant features are optional.** "Copy request for Claude" only copies text to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it into an AI service yourself, under that service's terms.

## HIPAA
The developer is not a covered entity or business associate and never receives health information. A clinic that recommends the app is not sharing data with it. Patients choose what to show or send their care team (printed report, CSV export).

## Questions
Open an issue on the repository. Please do not post personal health information in issues.
